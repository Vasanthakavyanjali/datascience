
# Motor Insurance Claims and Policy Analytics

## 1. Executive Summary

This project analyzes motor insurance customer, vehicle, policy, claim, and payment data. The objective is to understand policy portfolio performance, claim trends, premium collection, settlement activity, and potential business risks.

## 2. Business Objectives

- Understand the insurance policy portfolio.
- Monitor premium and coverage amounts.
- Analyze claim volumes, statuses, and claim amounts.
- Review payment and settlement performance.
- Identify trends and potential areas of business risk.
- Support data-driven insurance business decisions.

## 3. Datasets

The project uses five datasets:

- Customers: customer demographics and location.
- Vehicles: vehicle details and estimated values.
- Policies: policy periods, policy types, premiums, and coverage.
- Claims: accident details, claim amounts, and claim statuses.
- Payments: payment amounts, dates, methods, and statuses.

## 4. Key Performance Indicators

The analysis covers the following KPIs where the required data is available:

- Total customers
- Total policies
- Active, expired, and cancelled policies
- Total and average premium
- Average coverage amount
- Total claims
- Total and average claim amount
- Claim approval and rejection rates
- Average claim processing time
- Average settlement time
- Total and average payments
- Claim amount compared with premium and coverage

## 5. Data Quality

The data preparation process checks:

- Required columns
- Missing values
- Duplicate identifiers
- Data types and date formats
- Invalid or inconsistent records
- Relationships between customers, vehicles, policies, claims, and payments

Data quality issues should be reviewed before business conclusions are made.

## 6. Dashboard Overview

The Streamlit dashboard provides the following analysis sections:

- Executive Overview
- Policy Analysis
- Claims Analysis
- Customer Analysis
- Vehicle Analysis
- Premium and Risk
- Insights and Recommendations

Filters allow users to explore relevant subsets of the available data.

## 7. Business Insights

The final insights should be completed after analyzing the actual datasets. Important areas include:

- Policy status and portfolio composition
- Premium distribution
- Claim status and claim type patterns
- Claim amounts by vehicle category
- Payment and settlement trends
- Potentially unusual claim or premium patterns

Do not treat an observed association as proof of fraud or causation.

## 8. Recommendations

Potential recommendations, subject to validation against the actual results:

- Monitor claim processing and settlement delays.
- Review data quality issues before reporting KPIs.
- Track premium and coverage trends by policy type.
- Compare claim costs with premium and coverage amounts.
- Investigate unusual records using appropriate business review procedures.
- Review dashboard KPIs regularly to support operational decisions.

## 9. Conclusion

The project establishes a data analytics workflow for motor insurance operations, combining data validation, cleaning, KPI calculations, visualization, and dashboard reporting.

Final conclusions and numerical findings should be added after the actual datasets have been processed.
